Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who uses Cubed

Cubed is used only by its owner, the creator of the Okay Davey channel, to publish the owner's own videos. It has no other users.

What data Cubed accesses

When the owner connects an account, Cubed receives the access permissions the owner approves (for example, permission to upload videos and read basic profile information such as the account name). It uses them only to upload, schedule and confirm the owner's own posts.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. Cubed has no server and no database, and it does not collect analytics.

Sharing

Cubed does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Videos and their text are sent only to the platform the owner chose to post on (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook).

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect Cubed at any time by removing its access in each platform's account settings and deleting the local Cubed folder, which removes all stored tokens.

YouTube API Services

Cubed uses YouTube API Services. By using Cubed, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of YouTube data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

YouTube data Cubed uses: the channel name and ID of the signed-in owner's own channel (to confirm the right channel is connected), and the upload responses for videos the owner uploads (video ID and processing status). Cubed only calls videos.insert, thumbnails.set and channels.list (mine=true). It does not read, store or show any other user's YouTube data.

How it is used and shared: only to upload, schedule and confirm the owner's own videos. YouTube data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage: the Google OAuth token is stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. Cubed has no server and does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Deleting the local Cubed folder deletes all stored tokens and data immediately; anything Cubed stored is deleted within 7 days of access being revoked.

Other platforms

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook data is subject to those platforms' own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Okay Davey Facebook Page.
