Cubed

A personal publishing tool for the Okay Davey channel.

Cubed is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule the owner's own short videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook Page accounts in one step.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's accounts or data.

YouTube

Cubed uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the Okay Davey YouTube channel and set their thumbnails. By using Cubed you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

How Cubed handles data, including YouTube data: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy

TikTok

Cubed uses the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit to publish the owner's original animated shorts to the owner's own TikTok account, @okay.davey. Before every post, Cubed shows the TikTok account it will post to, a preview of the video, an editable title, and the creator's own choices for who can view the video, comments, Duet, Stitch and content disclosure. Nothing is preselected, and nothing is sent to TikTok until the creator confirms. Use of TikTok through Cubed is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.
