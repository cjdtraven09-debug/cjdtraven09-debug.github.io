Cubed

A personal publishing tool for the Okay Davey channel.

Cubed is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule the owner's own short videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook Page accounts in one step.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's accounts or data.
